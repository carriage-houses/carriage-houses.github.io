Terms and Conditions

Your Terms and Conditions section is like a contract between you and your customers. You'll want it to include your rules and regulations, the terms of service, and state that users must agree to them before using the site.

Terms typically cover matters such as:

  • Service withdrawals, cancellations, and financial matters
  • Customer expectations regarding liability and downtime
  • Copyright and usage permissions
  • Prohibition of unlawful conduct, hate speech, harassment, and spam
  • Account termination conditions
